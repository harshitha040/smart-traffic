Traffic Prediction System 🚦

📌 Dataset Column Descriptions

  • Coded Day: Numeric representation of the day (Mon = 1, ..., Sun = 7).
  • Zone: Represents different geographical zones for traffic collection.
  • Weather: Weather conditions (coded):
  • Temperature: Temperature of the zone on that day.
  • Traffic: Coded from 1 (less than 5 cars) to 5 (more than 50 cars).
